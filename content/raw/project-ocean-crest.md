Ocean Crest is a thoughtfully designed residential project that brings together modern architecture, comfort, and a refined lifestyle. Crafted with attention to detail and quality construction, the project offers elegant living spaces designed to provide both functionality and sophistication. Inspired by serene coastal surroundings and contemporary design principles, Ocean Crest creates a peaceful environment while keeping residents connected to essential urban conveniences.

With well-planned homes, beautiful surroundings, and a focus on quality living, Ocean Crest is ideal for families and individuals seeking a balance of luxury and tranquility. The project reflects a commitment to excellence, offering a lifestyle that combines comfort, style, and long-term value in a truly distinctive address.

### Villa Type A 170.7m² (1837 Sq.Ft)

##### Area of land

The total land area for this villa is 350 m² (3767 Sq.Ft.), offering ample outdoor space.

##### Area of villa

The built-up area of the villa is 170.7 m² (1837 sqft), providing a comfortable living environment. The property includes a spacious 2-car parking garage for convenient vehicle storage. It features a beautiful sea-view infinity pool, perfect for relaxation and scenic enjoyment. The villa is designed with 4 well-sized bedrooms to accommodate family living.

##### Optional

Optional additions include a home theatre or a fully equipped health club, depending on your preference.

##### Customisation

Customisation options are available on demand to suit individual needs and tastes. The villa comes with fully furnished interiors, designed for comfort and style.
