### ROYAL HABITAT

Occupying an expansive 20,000 sq. meter plot, Royal Habitat is a 10,000 sq. ft. architectural masterpiece featuring six grand bedrooms, a lavish home theatre, executive office, and a breathtaking indoor water fountain. The property is surrounded by a landscaped garden, fruit orchard, and private swimming pool — offering the perfect harmony of elegance and serenity. Royal Habitat is the ultimate symbol of prestige and modern royal comfort.
