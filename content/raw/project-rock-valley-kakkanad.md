### ROCK VALLEY KAKKANAD

Located in the heart of Kakkanad, Rock Valley spans 10,000 sq. meters with a 4,000 sq. ft. premium residence. The four-bedroom villa includes a private swimming pool, home theatre, office space, and a lush garden for leisure. Designed with contemporary aesthetics, it offers urban luxury within a peaceful natural setting — an ideal home for refined living.
