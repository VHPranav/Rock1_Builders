### MISTY BLUE

Nestled amidst the misty hills of Munnar, Misty Blue redefines luxury and tranquility. Spread across a beautifully landscaped garden, this five-star resort features 35 executive rooms, a sparkling swimming pool, wellness and spa center, multi-cuisine restaurant, and a dedicated barbeque zone. Guests can unwind by the private pond and serene lake, or enjoy cultural evenings at the open-air amphitheatre. With panoramic views of lush greenery, Misty Blue stands as Munnar’s first and only lake resort, blending nature and sophistication seamlessly.
